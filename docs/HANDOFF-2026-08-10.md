# Shadow Bets — Full System Handoff

**Written 2026-08-10.** Everything needed to pick this up cold. Read sections 1–4
before touching anything; they contain the two mistakes that will otherwise
cost you a week.

---

## 1. The one-paragraph version

Shadow Bets is an automated sports-betting model (MLB/NBA/NHL/NFL/EPL) built as
a solo side project. It generates picks on a schedule, locks them exactly once,
tracks each to the closing line, and grades them. Its purpose is to find out
whether the model has a real edge, measured primarily by **closing line value
(CLV)** rather than ROI, because ROI at this volume is almost pure noise.

**Honest state: the model does not currently have a profitable edge, and that
conclusion is trustworthy for the first time.** It beats the closing line by
about +0.77pp on MLB moneyline. It pays about 1.15pp in vig at the books the
operator actually has. Net: **roughly −0.38pp per bet.** Every prior number
suggesting otherwise was produced by a broken instrument.

## 2. THE TWO MISTAKES THAT WILL COST YOU A WEEK

### 2.1 Never query `performance_log` without a regime filter

The table contains two incompatible eras. `pick_regime='v1_daily'` is the old
process: the same game re-decided every morning, the side flipping as the line
moved, graded by a grader since fenced off for mis-grading. `pick_regime='v2_clv'`
is the current process.

Blending them produces confident nonsense. On 2026-08-09 an automated report
headlined **"Staked ROI: 19.5%"**. The split:

| Regime | decided | staked | ROI |
|---|---|---|---|
| `v1_daily` | 1240 | 415.9u | **+25.8%** |
| `v2_clv` | 303 | 110.8u | **−0.5%** |

80% of the rows behind that headline described a process that no longer exists.
**Always filter `pick_regime='v2_clv'`.** For CLV analysis also require
`clv_basis='novig'` AND `close_lag_hours <= 6`.

### 2.2 Never pool across `model_version`

The model changed materially on 2026-08-09 and again on 08-10. Rows from
different versions are different models and must not be averaged together.

| `model_version` | rows | dates | usable? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `v2.0-pre-feature-vocab` | 372 | Jul 31 – Aug 8 | historical only |
| `v2.2-corrupt-offense` | 42 | Aug 9 only | **NO — quarantined** |
| `v2.3-rate-gate-2026-08-09` | accumulating | Aug 10 → | **yes, this is the baseline** |

The 42 quarantined rows were produced while one corrupt feature contributed 89%
of the model's total score. They are retained for forensics, never for analysis.

## 4. The governing rules

**"Rows are tickets, not opinions."** Each game+market is picked exactly once,
locked early, tracked to close, graded by game date. No daily re-picking, no
chasing the other side. Enforced structurally: `pick_id = game_key:market`,
with side and pick_date deliberately excluded from the key, and a DB-level
unique index with `ignoreDuplicates: true`.

**CLV is the optimizer objective; ROI is a guardrail.** CLV is continuous and
readable within hours; ROI needs thousands of settled bets.

**Game identity is `event_id` + `game_number`.** Never `commence_time` — start
times move, and a timestamp in an identity key creates duplicate tickets.

**The staking gate demotes, it does not terminate.** Underperforming markets
drop to tracking stake (0.01u); they are never killed.

## 5. Where the numbers actually stand

Measured on `v2_clv` + `clv_basis='novig'` + `close_lag_hours <= 6`, approved
picks only, before the 08-09/08-10 model changes:

| Market | n | CLV | Vig @ Bovada+BetOnline | **Net edge** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **moneyline** | 57 | +0.765pp | 1.146pp | **−0.38pp** |
| spread | 48 | +0.434pp | 1.432pp | −1.00pp |
| totals | 44 | +0.760pp | 1.883pp | −1.12pp |

**Moneyline needs roughly 50% more CLV to break even.** Spread and totals need
to more than double.

Independently corroborated: v2 realized ROI is −0.5% on 110.8u staked. Two
different methods, same answer.

### Books matter enormously

The operator has **Bovada** (primary) and **BetOnline** (secondary), plus Novig
and DraftKings which are not usable for this. Cost of restricting to reachable
books vs unlimited line shopping:

| Book set | h2h | spreads | totals |
|---|---|---|---|
| bovada + betonlineag | −0.47pp | −0.51pp | −0.48pp |
| bovada alone | −1.52pp | −1.05pp | −1.43pp |
| bovada + draftkings | −1.20pp | −0.83pp | −0.82pp |

`config/books.json` restricts price selection to `["bovada","betonlineag"]`.
**Any measurement using best-price-across-all-books is fiction** — the best
price usually sits at lowvig/mybookieag/betus, which are not available.

### Novig is the structurally better venue, and unavailable

Novig is a no-vig peer-to-peer exchange: the two sides sum to 1, the cost is
the bid-ask spread rather than a 4.5% hold. On a no-vig venue the +0.77pp CLV
*is* the return. But Novig has no public API, is not carried by The Odds API,
and as of 2026-08-10 the operator **cannot reliably get filled there.** Revisit
if that changes — it would flip the economics entirely.

## 6. What was fixed 2026-08-08 → 08-10 (16 commits)

Every one of these was silent: green workflows, picks produced, wrong data.

**Measurement integrity**
1. **Spread close-side capture.** Odds were keyed `eventId|market|outcome` with
   no `point`. Books disagree on which side of a runline a team is on, so all
   prices pooled into one median while the point came from whichever row parsed
   first. 11 of 109 spread rows froze a price from one line wearing another
   line's label; CLV values reached −0.31, physically impossible. Now keyed
   including point, with a sign guard. Spread CLV moved −0.0116 → +0.0040.
2. **`placed_book`/`best_book`/`best_odds`** populated (were NULL on all rows),
   enabling the book-latency check.
3. **`vig_paid_pp`/`net_edge_pp`** columns added and backfilled.
4. **`config/books.json`** restricts pricing to reachable books.
5. **Clean-era ROI report** repointed at Supabase with a regime filter; it had
   been reading the Google Sheet, which has no `pick_regime` column.

**Model inputs** — the big one
6. **Weights referenced features that did not exist.** `scoreMarket()` does
   `features[key]`, gets `undefined`, skips silently. Coverage before → after:

| League | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| NBA | 100 / 100 / 100% | unchanged (reference implementation) |
| MLB | **43 / 45 / 50%** | 77 / 81 / 75% |
| NHL | 57 / 55 / 51% | 89 / 90 / 79% |
| NFL | 75 / 76 / 83% | 97 / 98 / 83% |

7. **Four "recent form windows" were one number scaled** — `formDiff × {1.0,
   1.1, 1.2, 1.3}`, correlation exactly 1.0, carrying ~1.65 combined weight and
   ~90% of the moneyline signal. This is why the MLB deep sweep found a "flat
   weight space": every weight vector over four copies of one variable reduces
   to a scalar. Now real L1/L3/L5 from an ordered sequence.
8. **Recent form sampled 5 non-consecutive dates** out of 14, gated at ≥3
   games, so `formDiff` took three values (−0.5, 0, +0.5). Now walks
   consecutive days keeping order.
9. **MLB run differential read season totals as per-game rates.** ESPN provides
   *no* per-game average for runs — only `batting.runs` (537), `pitching.runs`
   (532) and `gamesPlayed` (119). The old code took `stats['runs']` first: a
   season total, and ambiguous between batting and pitching because the stat
   flattener collapsed all categories into one namespace. Now derives
   total ÷ gamesPlayed first, with category-prefixed keys.
10. **New data collected that was weighted but never gathered:** MLB team OPS,
    batting average, WHIP, starter WHIP; NFL turnover margin, total/opponent/
    pass/rush yards, third-down %, red-zone %.

**Guards**
11. **`src/feature-health.js`** — dead weights, duplicated weight (correlation
    ≥0.99), collapsed features. Wired into `trigger16`.
12. **`checkDominance()`** — flags any feature at ≥40% of total absolute
    contribution. Reports, never clamps.
13. **Range gates** on scoring rates and run-diff inputs.
14. **`src/debug-probe.js`** + `debug_probe` table (see §7).

### The headline result

`model_prob` on 2026-08-08: **4 distinct values across all 15 games, every
day** — a confidence-tier lookup, not a probability.
On 2026-08-10: **30 distinct values across 30 picks**, range 0.352–0.806.

## 7. The development loop (important — this was the real bottleneck)

**GitHub Actions logs are NOT readable from the Claude sandbox.** Log downloads
come from `results-receiver.actions.githubusercontent.com`, outside the egress
allowlist. Run metadata (status, steps, conclusions) *is* reachable via
`api.github.com`. So `console.log` is write-only for debugging — which is
exactly backwards for a system whose failure mode is jobs succeeding while
producing wrong data. A diagnostic printed on 08-09 was unreadable and the bug
survived another full day.

**Therefore: anything needed to DIAGNOSE goes to `debug_probe`, not stdout.**
`console.log` stays for narration.

```js
const { probe, probeKeys } = require('./debug-probe');
await probeKeys('data-collection', 'mlb-espn-run-keys', stats, /run|avg/i, { team: abbr });
```

Then query `SELECT * FROM debug_probe ORDER BY created_at DESC`.

**The working loop, which takes about 4 minutes:**
1. Edit + push to `main`
2. `POST /repos/nickciesinski/ShadowB/actions/workflows/<name>.yml/dispatches`
   with `{"ref":"main"}` (needs a PAT with `workflow` scope)
3. Poll `/actions/workflows/<name>.yml/runs?event=workflow_dispatch` until
   `completed`
4. Query `debug_probe` / `prediction_features` for the result

A `trigger2` dispatch completes in ~45 seconds. This replaced waiting a full
day per iteration and is the single biggest quality-of-life improvement
available.

**Optional upgrade:** adding `results-receiver.actions.githubusercontent.com`
to Settings → Capabilities → Code execution → Additional allowed domains would
allow raw log reads. Note the allowlist is baked into a sandbox at session
start, so it requires a **new conversation** to take effect. There is also a
known bug where additional domains are ignored while the mode is "Package
managers only".

- **E7 — re-baseline.** At ~800 rows of `model_version LIKE 'v2.3%'` (around

  `logit(outcome) ~ β₁·logit(model_prob) + β₂·logit(close_novig_prob)`. This
  will be the first time that test measures a model that can actually see.
  β₁ ≈ 0 means the model adds nothing beyond the close and effort should move
  to selection and timing. β₁ > 0 significant means Phase 7 is worth building.
- **Phase C readout.** Does locking at 9:30 PM beat locking at 2 AM, with the
  pick held fixed? Preliminary evidence is flat but confounded (all existing
  variation comes from game start times, not lock times).
- **E8 — recalibrate `confidenceToUnits()`.** Only meaningful now that
  `model_prob` is a real probability.
- **E6 — audit NBA/NFL/NHL more deeply.** Out of season is the cheapest time.
- **Phase D — staking gate.** LAST. It promotes and demotes on measured segment
  performance; 08-10 reset the clock on every measurement. Design: rolling net

  shrinkage toward the pool, segment at market level only until a league passes

## 10. Working practices that matter here

- **A green workflow is not success.** This is the documented #1 risk and it is
  how every bug in §6 survived. Verify against production before and after.
- **Diagnose in plain language before building.** Then verify the fix against
  real data, not against an assumption about what the upstream API returns.
- **Gate inputs, not just outputs.** A gate on a computed result catches gross
  corruption but lets through garbage that lands inside the band. On 08-10, 24
  of 30 games were correctly zeroed while 6 passed a plausible-looking value
  into a weight of 1.7.
- **`scoreMarket()` is an unbounded linear sum with no clamping anywhere.** Any
  out-of-range feature silently becomes the entire model.
- **A feature can be wrong for months and only become dangerous when something
  finally reads it.** `mlb_run_diff` sat at −5825 harmlessly until an alias
  connected it to weight 1.2.
- **Deliver complete function replacements, not diffs.** The operator is not a
  strong coder and works by copy-paste.
- **Python string-replacement patch pattern:** assert `s.count(old)==1` before
  replacing, then `node --check` immediately. Watch for mojibake bytes in older
  comments, which break exact-match anchors.
- **Git:** `git push --force-with-lease` with the PAT inline, sanitised from
  output via `sed`. Automated workflows commit to `main` mid-session; when that
  happens `git rebase FETCH_HEAD` resolves it cleanly.

## 11. Verification checklist for any new change

```sql
-- 1. headline: sample size and net edge (THE dashboard)
SELECT COUNT(*) n,
       ROUND(AVG(clv_prob_delta)::numeric,5)  mean_clv,
       ROUND(AVG(vig_paid_pp)::numeric,5)     mean_vig,
       ROUND(AVG(net_edge_pp)::numeric,5)     net_edge,
       ROUND((AVG(net_edge_pp)/NULLIF(STDDEV(net_edge_pp),0)*SQRT(COUNT(*)))::numeric,2) t_stat
FROM performance_log
WHERE pick_regime='v2_clv' AND clv_basis='novig' AND close_lag_hours <= 6
  AND model_version LIKE 'v2.3%';

-- 2. data health — regressions here invalidate everything above
SELECT game_date, COUNT(*) n,
       ROUND(AVG(close_lag_hours)::numeric,2) avg_lag,
       COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE close_lag_hours < 0) neg_lag,
       COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE market='spread' AND SIGN(line::numeric)<>SIGN(close_line::numeric)) side_flip,
       COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE ABS(clv_prob_delta) > 0.10) impossible_clv,
       COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE status='closed' AND graded_at IS NULL) ungraded
FROM performance_log WHERE pick_regime='v2_clv' AND game_date >= CURRENT_DATE - 7
GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 1;

-- 3. is the model differentiating games? (was 4 distinct values for 15 games)
SELECT market, COUNT(*) n, COUNT(DISTINCT ROUND(model_prob::numeric,4)) distinct_probs
FROM performance_log
WHERE pick_regime='v2_clv' AND game_date >= CURRENT_DATE GROUP BY 1;

-- 4. is one feature eating the model?
SELECT c->>'feature' AS feature,
       ROUND(AVG(ABS((c->>'contribution')::numeric)),3) AS avg_abs_contrib
FROM prediction_features pf, jsonb_array_elements(pf.top_contributions) c
WHERE pf.league='MLB' AND pf.date >= CURRENT_DATE
GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 2 DESC LIMIT 8;
```

**Expected healthy:** `distinct_probs` ≈ row count; no single feature more than
~2–3× the next; `side_flip`, `impossible_clv`, `neg_lag`, `ungraded` all zero.

## 12. What "done" looks like

The operator's goal is a system that runs without attention and improves on its
own toward an achievable target. Concretely:

> At least one league × market × window segment sustains **net edge > +0.005**
> over 250+ tickets, with the staking gate holding it at full stake while
> everything else runs at tracking stake.

+0.005 is roughly 0.5% ROI — small, real, defensible. Moneyline is currently at
−0.0038, so this is not fantasy; it is roughly one good improvement away.

**If nothing reaches it after a full accumulation cycle, that is also a valid
end state.** A working measurement rig plus an honest null is a real result,
and much better than a flattering number produced by a broken instrument —
which is precisely what this system had until 2026-08-08.
