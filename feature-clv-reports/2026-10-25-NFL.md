Every figure is **clustered by game** and every value is turned to **the side we picked** (positive = the feature favoured our pick). Sides (moneyline + spread) and totals are reported separately — a home-minus-away difference says nothing about over vs under. Reports before 2026-10-01 did none of the last three and understated real effects (starter_ip_l3_diff: t=+0.26 then, t=+3.49 corrected).

> **The two eras below are not comparable and are never pooled.** On 2026-08-31 `top_contributions` changed from the top 5 features to all 27. Before that date a feature is only present when it ranked top-5, which selects on the same magnitude being correlated; after it, every feature is present in every game. Pooling the two is what made run_differential_diff read t=3.64 on 2026-09-10.
