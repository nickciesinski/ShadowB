## CLV — closing-line value (R1.1)

CLV = market move toward our side after we bet (our odds vs closing odds). **This is the leading indicator** — it gives signal on every bet immediately, unlike W/L. Sustained positive CLV on staked bets is the durable proof the edge is real, not luck. Rows without a closing snapshot are excluded from CLV counts.

## Price class — plus money vs laying juice

Staked bets only. **Net edge = CLV − vig, which is expected ROI per unit staked** — it is measured on every ticket, so it answers "is this segment worth money?" without waiting for win/loss variance. Books typically hold about twice as much on the favourite side, so this split usually separates the profitable half of the book from the rest.
